Designing Two New Construction Apartment Buildings in Indianapolis

Designing apartment spaces has evolved into a cornerstone of our expertise. When Morris Cohen approached us to design two new construction apartment buildings, we knew it was an opportunity to showcase our collaborative approach and commitment to efficiency. Coordinating with structural engineers, and contractors, we embarked on a journey to turn vision into reality, balancing cost-effectiveness with functionality.


Completing the Triad: Electrical, Plumbing, and Mechanical Design


At the heart of any building lie its core systems, and we spared no effort in meticulously planning every detail. The electrical, plumbing, and mechanical systems formed the backbone of our design, engineered to deliver seamless performance and efficiency.


Electrical Design

Safety and reliability were paramount considerations in our electrical layout. From conducting meticulous load calculations to installing 200-amp single-phase panels, every aspect was carefully crafted to meet modern living standards while prioritizing safety.


Plumbing Design

Efficient water management was a key focus of our plumbing design. We optimized flow while minimizing waste, incorporating advanced drainage systems and state-of-the-art fixtures to maximize resident comfort and convenience.


Mechanical Design

Comfort was central to our HVAC planning. We meticulously integrated a 2-ton HVAC system to ensure optimal climate control year-round, enhancing residents' living experience.


Sustainability at the Core

In an era where environmental consciousness is crucial, sustainability guided our design philosophy. Energy-efficient lighting, water-saving fixtures, and eco-friendly materials were seamlessly integrated, minimizing the environmental footprint while meeting residents' needs.


Approval

In a testament to our efficiency, our plans received approval from the city in just two months—a remarkable feat for a development project. With construction underway, we anticipate completion by early 2025, staying true to our commitment to timely and quality delivery.


Conclusion

Designing a 30-unit apartment building demands dedication and expertise, factors we've honed over years of practice. From meticulous electrical, plumbing, and mechanical design to our unwavering commitment to sustainability, every aspect was geared toward creating spaces residents would love while saving our client costs and construction headaches.
