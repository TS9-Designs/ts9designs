What we draw in Illinois

  • Building design and drafting. Floor plans, elevations, sections and site plans for new homes, additions, remodels and commercial spaces.
  • MEP engineering. Mechanical, electrical and plumbing drawings, coordinated with each other before you submit.
  • Structural engineering. Framing, foundations, beams and the calculations behind them, when the work needs it.
  • Fire protection and fire alarm. Sprinkler and alarm drawings for commercial and multifamily projects.
  • Permit documentation. Stamped and sealed sets, with help answering the reviewer's comments.

What Illinois reviewers look at

Building codes in Illinois vary a lot from one town to the next. Chicago has its own Chicago Construction Codes, including the 2019 Chicago Building Code based on the 2018 International Building Code. Outside Chicago, each city or county adopts its own codes, and the editions in use differ widely.

Energy is the exception: the Illinois Energy Conservation Code applies statewide, and its latest edition, based on the 2024 IECC with Illinois amendments, took effect November 30, 2025. We confirm the local building code before we draw.

What to send us

  • The property address and what you want to build.
  • Photos of the space or lot, or a rough sketch.
  • The rough size in square feet.
  • Any existing plans or a survey, if you have them.

That's enough for a price and a start date. Plans start at $0.45/sq ft and most take 4–10 days.
