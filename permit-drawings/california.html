What California reviewers look at

California uses its own Building Standards Code, Title 24. The 2025 edition applies to permit applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026.

Energy compliance is a big part of review. The energy code (Title 24, Part 6) covers new buildings, additions and many alterations, and the 2025 update encourages heat pumps for space and water heating. Lighting, HVAC and water heating all have to show they meet it.

CALGreen (Part 11) adds green-building requirements on top, and many cities have their own local amendments, so we check those before we draw.

What to send us

  • The property address and what you want to build.
  • Photos of the space or lot, or a rough sketch.
  • The rough size in square feet.
  • Any existing plans or a survey, if you have them.

That's enough for a price and a start date. Plans start at $0.45/sq ft and most take 4–10 days.
