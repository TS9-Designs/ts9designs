How We Work

A clear path from first call to permit-ready delivery.

Every project moves through the same disciplined process, so you always know what's next.

01

Consultation & Scope Discovery

You reach out through our contact form, email, or phone — we schedule a consultation to understand your project's scope and requirements.

02

Estimate & Proposal

We provide a permit-ready cost estimate based on your project type, square footage, and scope.

03

Design & Engineering

Our team produces MEP, structural, and fire protection drawings — typically within 4-10 days.

04

Internal QA & Coordination

Before issue, we run a cross-discipline review to catch clashes and keep schedules, notes, and calculations consistent.

05

Permit Submission & Delivery

You receive a code-compliant, permit-ready package structured for a smooth plan review.
