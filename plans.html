Plan Description

TS9Designs proudly provided the full engineering plans for the Bishop Pond residential community in Indianapolis a beautifully planned development combining modern living with classic Midwestern charm.

Our team delicered comprehesive Mechanical, electrical, Electrical, and Plumbing(MEP) engineering designed for efficiency, comfort, and long-term reliability, aligning with the builder's vision and local code requirements.

From concept to permit-ready plans, our engineering support helped bring elegant neighbohood to life another example of how TS9Designs powers great design through smart engineering.
