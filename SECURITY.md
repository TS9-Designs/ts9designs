# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security problem in this website or its
contact-form worker, please email **info@ts9designs.com** with the details
(what you found, where, and steps to reproduce).

- Please do not publicly disclose the issue until we have had a chance to fix it.
- We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days.
- Please do not access, change or delete other people's data, and do not run
  automated attacks or denial-of-service tests against the live site.

## Scope

- `www.ts9designs.com` (static site hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare)
- The contact and estimate form worker in `mailer-worker/`

Third-party services we use (GitHub, Cloudflare, Resend, cal.com, Stripe) have
their own disclosure programs; report issues in those products to the vendor.
