Who These Terms Cover

These terms apply when you buy a pre-designed home plan from ts9designs, operated by TSABA Corp., including the MEP Plans Add-on. By completing a purchase, you agree to them.

Custom design and engineering projects are covered by the proposal we send you for that project. Consultations are covered by the fee credit and cancellation policies on our Contact page.

What You Receive

Each plan includes the drawings listed under "What's Included" on that plan's details. If you add the MEP Plans Add-on, the mechanical, electrical and plumbing drawings are included as well.

  • Format: A PDF drawing set, sent by email
  • Timing: Within 1–2 business days of payment, including the MEP Plans Add-on
  • Where it goes: The email address used at checkout

If your files haven't arrived within 2 business days, check your spam folder, then contact us.

Your License to Build

Buying a plan gives you a license to build one home from it. Buying a quantity of two or more licenses that many homes.

  • You may: Print and share the drawings with your builder, lender, consultants and building department for that home
  • You may not: Build more homes from the plan than you've purchased, resell the drawings, or share them publicly
  • Ownership: TSABA Corp. keeps the copyright in all drawings; your purchase is a license to use them, not a transfer of ownership

To build the plan again, buy it again or contact us.

Stamps, Local Codes and Changes

Pre-designed plans are delivered as designed. They are not stamped by a licensed professional, and the price does not include adapting them to your local building code or site.

  • Local review: Building requirements vary by jurisdiction. Confirm what your building department requires before you build
  • Stamping and adaptation: Available as a separate, quoted service, performed by or under the responsible charge of a professional licensed in your state
  • Changes: No changes are included in the plan price. Any change to a plan is quoted separately

To request a quote, email info@ts9designs.com or use our Contact page.

Prices and Payment

Prices are shown in U.S. dollars on each plan. Single-plan purchases are paid securely through Stripe. Orders with more than one plan, more than one copy, or other requests are confirmed with you through our Contact page before payment.

Refunds

You can cancel for a full refund any time before your drawings are sent. Email info@ts9designs.com with your order details.

Because drawings are digital files that can't be returned, purchases are not refundable once the files have been sent.
