Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we protect and handle your information when you work with TS9 Designs.

Last Updated: January 2025

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At TS9 Designs, we understand that trust is the foundation of every great design partnership. When you share your project vision, building plans, or business information with us, we treat it with the same care and precision we bring to our building-design, MEP, structural, and fire protection solutions.

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or work with our design team.

Information We Collect

To deliver exceptional design services, we may collect:

  • Project Information: Building specifications, site plans, design requirements, and project timelines
  • Contact Details: Name, email, phone number, and company information
  • Communication Records: Project discussions, design feedback, and consultation notes
  • Website Analytics: How you interact with our portfolio and service pages
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information for website optimization

How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us deliver the design excellence you expect:

  • Project Delivery: Creating building-design, MEP, structural, and fire protection solutions tailored to your needs
  • Communication: Keeping you updated on project progress and design milestones
  • Service Improvement: Enhancing our design processes and client experience
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting building codes, safety regulations, and professional standards
  • Portfolio Development: Showcasing completed projects (with your permission)

How We Protect Your Information

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your data:

  • Secure Storage: Encrypted databases and secure cloud storage for project files
  • Access Control: Limited access to authorized team members only
  • Professional Standards: Adherence to professional design, engineering, and confidentiality practices
  • Regular Updates: Ongoing security assessments and system updates
  • Secure Communication: Encrypted email and secure file sharing for sensitive project data

Information Sharing

We respect your privacy and only share information when necessary:

  • Project Partners: With contractors, consultants, or authorities as required for project completion
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, building codes, or regulatory authorities
  • Service Providers: With trusted vendors who help us deliver our services (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a business sale or merger (with advance notice)

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your information:

  • Access: Request copies of the information we have about you
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request removal of your information (subject to legal and contractual obligations)
  • Portability: Receive your project data in a standard format
  • Communication Preferences: Opt out of marketing communications while maintaining project updates

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality and security
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our portfolio
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

Data Retention

We retain your information based on:

  • Active Projects: Throughout the project lifecycle and warranty period
  • Completed Projects: For 7 years to meet professional liability requirements
  • Marketing Communications: Until you opt out or request deletion
  • Legal Requirements: As required by applicable laws and regulations

Policy Updates

As our design services evolve and privacy laws change, we may update this policy. We'll notify you of significant changes through:

  • Email notifications to active clients
  • Website announcements
  • Project communication updates

Continued use of our services after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions About Your Privacy?

Our team is here to address any privacy concerns or questions about how we handle your information.
