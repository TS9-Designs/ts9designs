Services

We specialize in providing Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) and Structural plans for residential and commercial projects. Whether you're starting a new construction or renovating an existing space, we can help with comprehensive engineering plans.

Process

Simply fill out our contact form on the website or reach out to us directly via email or phone. Once we have an idea of your project needs, we'll schedule a consultation to discuss your requirements in detail.

To get started, we typically need basic information about your project, such as the size of the space, the scope of work (new construction, renovation, etc.), and any specific requirements or design preferences you may have. The more details you can provide, the better we can tailor our services to meet your needs.

The timeline for completing engineering plans typically takes 4-10 days to complete.

Technical

Yes, all our engineering plans are created by professionals and comply with local building codes and regulations. We ensure that your project meets all necessary standards to pass inspections and obtain permits.

ts9designs provides design, drafting, engineering, and project coordination services within the scope permitted by the applicable jurisdiction. When a project requires professional services outside ts9designs' permitted scope, those services must be provided through appropriately licensed professionals or firms in accordance with applicable state requirements.
